textbook-canonical-sourcesMay a textbook-canonical T0 case be built without its primary paper?not blockingstanding decision
raised 2026-07-31

About twenty P1/T0 cases are on the papers list only because find_papers could not reach a journal PDF, while the result itself is a closed form no two sources disagree about — Langmuir's isotherm, Michaelis–Menten, Chapman–Enskog, Knudsen/Bosanquet, the Stefan tube, Monod, Flory–Schulz. May these be built citing a standard monograph as the source, or do you want the original paper for every one of them?

The published-work-only policy is about manuscripts in preparation, so a monograph does satisfy it; the real risk is different. The builder brief forbids "textbook restatement passed off as the source's", and a page written from memory would be exactly that — it is the fabrication route for cases like these, because the model is remembered rather than read. So this is only safe with a named book actually to hand. If you have Bird, Stewart & Lightfoot, Taylor & Krishna, Levenspiel, Froment & Bischoff or Ruthven as PDFs, dropping them into ~/papers/pymrm-gallery/ would unblock the whole class at once and would be worth more than any single paper on the papers dashboard. A useful part of this class needs no such decision, and is being taken first: where a paper already on disk states the relation, that paper is the source. B1.6 (Prater's relation) is the example — Weisz & Hicks 1962, already on disk for B1.1/B1.5, prints it as their eq. 7 and attributes it to Prater.
