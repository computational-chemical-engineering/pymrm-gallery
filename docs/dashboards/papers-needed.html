A4.8Stefan tube / Arnold diffusionP1
—

WHY THE 2013 PAPER DOES NOT CLOSE THIS CASE. Read this before requesting again, and before assuming the case is served. A4.8 is catalogued as the "classic MS validation EXPERIMENT" - the whole point of the case is that Maxwell-Stefan is tested against measurements that Fick's law cannot reproduce, the same argument that makes A4.9 (Duncan-Toor) the strongest early page in the gallery. Mills & Chang is not that. It is a BINARY problem (water vapour evaporating into air), solved in TWO dimensions with the Navier-Stokes equations plus a Kramers-Kistemaker diffusion-slip wall condition, and its purpose is to rebut Salcedo-Diaz et al. (2008) and the Kerkhof-Geboers "Unified Theory". It is a polemic between two modelling frameworks. It contains NO experimental data and NO tables of any kind. Every result - axial and radial velocity profiles, the pressure field, species mass fluxes, vapour mass fraction, absolute air velocity - is a figure (Figs. 1-9). So even the model-versus-model comparison it does make is only reproducible by digitising figures, which parks at the figure-review gate that currently has no maintainer. What it IS good for, and what should be recorded rather than rediscovered: it states the one-dimensional classical solution ("diffusion with one component stationary") and its history - Lee & Wilke (1954) and Heinzelmann et al. (1965) on end effects, McDonald et al. (1971) and Meyer & Kostin (1975) on the first 2-D solutions with a no-slip wall - and it quantifies the diffusion-slip velocity for water into air as "at most about 25% of the bulk velocity". If A4.8 is ever built as a 1-D-versus-2-D page rather than a model-versus-data page, this is its source.
