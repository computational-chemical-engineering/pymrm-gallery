# Learnings from refreshing the published pages

One row per finding made while refreshing a page under
[`refresh-plan.md`](refresh-plan.md). A finding belongs here when it would change
what an agent writes or checks when building a pymrm model, and it carries evidence:
the page, and the numbers that showed it.

Targets in the pymrm repo (`plugins/pymrm/` unless stated):
`pitfall` (`skills/conventions/references/pitfalls.md`, with its test in
`pymrm/test/test_pitfalls.py`), `api-map`, `style-guide`, `numerics`, `exemplar`,
`eval` (a new case under `evals/`, usually built from the page), `verifier`
(`skills/verify-model/references/attack-list.md`), `library` (a pymrm bug or a missing
API; file it against `pymrm/src/pymrm/`).

Status: `open` (recorded), `filed` (in a pymrm PR, give the number), `merged`, or
`dropped` (with the reason).

Flush open rows to pymrm as one PR per batch of about ten refreshed pages.

| 6 | 2026-10-29 | `J4.8` | Never divide by a round-off quantity, not even in a printed sentence: a closure that is `7e-12` on one BLAS kernel is exactly `0.0` on another, and "better by a factor of" raised `ZeroDivisionError`. State the improvement against the bound the quantity is held to. | `reactor_cod_leak_exact_factors` 7.3e-12 (Haswell), 0.0 (Sandybridge), 3.6e-12 (Nehalem) | `style-guide`, `verifier` | open |
| 7 | 2026-10-29 | `J4.6` | A break row only protects a claim if it fails that claim's own check. Under CI's 5 % tolerance, a 1 % defect (`k2 + 1 %`) or a fit that moves a few per mille is invisible, so the old rule "some row moves the metric" passed metrics no check could catch. Size the row against the tolerance, or tighten an admissible tolerance (100 times the measured spread) until the row fails it. | migration: fit and root-find values got `rtol` 1e-5 to 1e-3 so existing rows fail them; five claims still have no failing row | `style-guide`, `verifier` | open |
| 8 | 2026-10-29 | `D1.1` | Cold-starting each bisection solve from a simpler model's profile is not neutral near a threshold: between the simpler model's threshold and the real one, that guess is already a runaway, and whether Newton then lands on the ignited state or fails (and a fallback ramp finds the lower state) is decided by round-off. The bisection classified noise, and the page published the noise as physics ("speckled" acceptance along a sweep, an "ignition jump" at Pe = 0.30, 13 of 24 probes refused). Locate a threshold by following the branch: one cold solve at a safe parameter value, then warm-start every scan point and midpoint from the converged state at the current lower end. Damping does not choose between multiple solutions (pymrm `newton` docstring). | rung 2, n_z = 900: direct starts at p0 = 0.01817, 0.01818, 0.01819, 0.01820 gave 990.9 C, 411.5 C, failure, 992.1 C; branch-following gives p0* = 0.018217101965 bit-identical on Haswell, Sandybridge, Nehalem (think, 2026-10-29) | `numerics`, `pitfall`, `verifier` | open |
| 9 | 2026-10-29 | `J1.10` | A fitted order taken from differences of nearly equal numbers on the pre-asymptotic end of a sequence moves with the numerical stack, not only with round-off: 1.79 in the August environment, 2.11 now, identical on three BLAS kernels and under pymrm 2.5.0, 3.0.1, 3.0.2 and 3.1.0 with the same numpy/scipy. Kernel variation does not reveal this class; only an environment change does. Report such an order as a range with its reason, and test that a break row still leaves the range. | `pymrm_dt_order_mu1` 1.79087 (2026-08-09) against 2.10895 (lock, 2026-10-29); backward-Euler row 0.91 then, 0.65 now | `style-guide`, `verifier` | open |
